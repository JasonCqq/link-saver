LinkStorage Browser Extension

When you use the LinkStorage browser extension, LinkStorage collects the URLs of browser tabs that you choose to save. These URLs may contain search queries or other information included in the URL.

The selected tab URLs are sent to LinkStorage's servers and are used to save and organize your links in your LinkStorage account.

The extension does not collect or send the URLs of tabs that you have not chosen to save.

The extension may also use an authentication token stored locally in your browser to securely connect the extension to your LinkStorage account. This token is used only to authenticate requests to LinkStorage.
